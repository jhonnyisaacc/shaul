# Completed review coverage and source gaps

All **81 existing notes** were substantively reviewed: **49 Yojanan** and
**32 Revelation**. This inventory covers the files that existed at the start of
review, including the nine integrated dossiers in the chapter-12 Yojanan study.
No new book or missing chapter coverage is implied. All revisions are in
[draft PR #38](https://github.com/jhonnyisaacc/shaul/pull/38).

The review checked lesson attribution, textual sequence, comparison quotations,
lexical claims, historical qualifications, and cross-links. All 66 studies with
registered public videos pass the transcript-quality and metadata gates. The
22 previously missing Eric maps are repaired. Existing video IDs are preserved.
The 15 raw Revelation notes received separate provenance treatment: fourteen
match blocks in the raw class-notes document; the older chapter-1 note has no
identified author or lesson and receives no invented video attribution.

Editorial review is complete. Research questions remain explicit in the linked
notes. Those questions do not turn an attributed classroom interpretation into
an established lexical, historical, or textual fact.

## Outstanding source questions

- **Lesson provenance:** identify the author or class behind
  [the older Revelation 1 note](../content/besorah/apocalipsis_1.md). Fourteen other
  raw studies have a document source but no identified video for their blocks.
- **Historical and rabbinic claims:** exact sources are still needed for temple
  chronology and practice, reconstructed tribunals and two thrones, historical
  figures, and proposed backgrounds. The notes retain those claims as attributed
  readings or pending questions.
- **Lexical and symbolic applications:** classroom uses of Abba, mitzvah, Ben
  Adam, numbers and durations, beasts, blood, and judgment must be distinguished
  from dictionary definitions. Greek identifications corrected in the review are
  checked against the publisher's SBLGNT; broader etymologies remain open where
  a primary lexical source is absent.
- **Corpus coverage:** OE and Delitzsch supply many anchors absent from TTH.
  Missing TTH is not a claim that the passage lacks a local biblical witness.
  The table below records missing TTH encountered in single-verse comparison
  cells; it is not an inventory of every possible passage or range.
- **Corpus integrity:** the local TTH Luke JSON repeats chapter numbers 1–21.
  This review used the first occurrence, matching the existing lookup helper.
  No corpus file was modified to resolve that upstream data issue.

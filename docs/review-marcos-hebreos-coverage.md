# Completed Marcos and Hebrews review coverage

All **34 existing notes** were reviewed: **21 Marcos** (16 canonical chapters
and five topical studies) and **13 Hebrews**. The revisions extend the completed
81-note Yojanan/Revelation stage in the same [draft PR #38](https://github.com/jhonnyisaacc/shaul/pull/38)
and `feat/review-notes` branch. No new book coverage, merge or deployment is implied.

The review checked local Scripture quotations, textual order, classroom
attribution, lexical forms, historical qualifications and cross-links. Repeated
generated controls were consolidated into the existing exposition. Every
pre-existing video-ID assignment is preserved. Chapters 5–6 of Hebrews now name
the verified shared part-6 source; the Marcos word/throne/seed study names the
verified part-8 source alongside its retained part-2 source.

There are **63 distinct registered public videos**. **55 have archived captions**;
**eight remain unavailable after a fresh recovery attempt** with the repository
transcript tool and its yt-dlp fallback. Caption presence is not certification of
automatic transcription or proof of every classroom assertion. The glossary is
reviewed through its canonical chapter-9 source instead of duplicating its ID.

Exact external references remain pending in the notes: rabbinic/legal practice,
Abba and prayer etymologies, occupation and place names, philosophical claims,
Daniel chronology and its named commentators, Gospel manuscript distributions,
and calendrical reconstructions. These are retained classroom readings and
research questions, not established lexical or historical conclusions. The
publisher’s [SBLGNT](https://github.com/Faithlife/SBLGNT) was used to verify Greek
forms and distinguish witnesses; it does not by itself establish manuscript
history. Scripture quotations use the local OE, TTH and Delitzsch families.

The local TTH corpus lacks numerous Hebrews and other comparison anchors;
available Delitzsch/OE text is retained rather than labeling all local Scripture
missing. Daniel 9 is quoted from OE. Psalm numbering follows the stated witness
(for example TTH 22:1 = OE 22:2). The inherited duplicate-chapter TTH Luke data
uses the existing lookup helper’s first-occurrence behavior. No Scripture corpus,
raw captions or private data is committed.
